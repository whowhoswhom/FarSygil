## Training analytics

- Banister impulse-response model (ATL/CTL/TSB): Banister et al. 1975
- "Performance Management Chart": popularised by Andrew Coggan and Joe Friel
- Riegel race predictor: Riegel, P.S. (1981). "Athletic records and human endurance". American Scientist. 69 (3): 285–290.
- Training Stress Score (TSS): Coggan, A. (2003)
